# Theory of Constraints Analysis

**Source:** Session 10 dry-run (2026-07-05, Claude Sonnet). Full analysis in fable5/research/.
**Confidence:** Medium

## The Constraint

**Attention** — not time, not revenue, not pipeline.

Structural fact: solo operator with 6+ active repos, each demanding context. Context switching costs eat delivery capacity. No mechanism to say no to new repos.

## 5 Whys Root Cause

1. Side projects stall at 60% → Context switching costs eat delivery
2. Context switching costs → No prioritization framework enforced
3. No prioritization framework → No mechanism to say no to new repos
4. No mechanism to say no → Build impulse overrides sell discipline
5. Build impulse overrides sell → Identity tied to shipping, not revenue

## Constraint Chain

Attention → Context switching → Delivery stalls → Revenue delayed → Cash flow depends on SMW retainer → Vulnerable to single-client concentration

## Action Plan

| Step | What to Do | Which Repo/Tool | Impact | Time | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Freeze all new repos for 30 days | All | Stops constraint widening | Immediate | # of active repos (no new ones) |
| 2 | Complete Sessions 13+14 (production workflow) | RaapTech OS, Agent Arcade | Ships real deliverables, not prototypes | 2 days | Merged PRs |
| 3 | Convert SMW project to retainer | SMW Cloud | Recurring revenue without per-project attention | 1 week | MRR from SMW |
| 4 | Build Agent Arcade to revenue (not prototype) | smw-cloud/agent-arcade | Second revenue stream | 2 weeks | First paying user |
| 5 | Route all maintenance to cheaper models | Hermes, Claude CLI | Free Fable quota for deep work | Ongoing | Fable quota usage |

## Next Constraint (After Relief)

Converting Agent Arcade from prototype to revenue-generating product — requires shipping and selling, not building.
