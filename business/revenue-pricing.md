# Revenue & Pricing

## Business Model

RaapTech LLC = solo AI/automation consultancy (Kyle Raap).

### Primary Revenue: Sheet Metal Werks Inc (SMW)
- Project/retainer mix
- Web platform: `smw-cloud-main`
- Active engagement — highest immediate ops leverage

### Secondary Revenue (Pipeline)
- **Agent Arcade** — Agent-skill marketplace MVP (prototype stage)
- **AI Trading Council** — Crypto trading (research mode, HL primary)
- **Consulting engagements** — Ad-hoc AI/automation work

### Internal (Cost Centers, Not Revenue)
- RaapTech OS — Operating system for the consultancy itself
- Fable 5 — Prompt discipline / one-shot execution repo
- RaapTech Brain — Knowledge bundle (this repo)
- The Pit — Trading research
- DontBuyJunkTech — Affiliate site (early stage)

## Pricing Principles

- Convert project work to retainer → recurring revenue without per-project attention overhead
- Route routine work to cheaper models → preserve expensive model quota for deep work
- Every repo needs a revenue path or it's a cost center — evaluate honestly
